Jack Perry

Senior, Wake Forest University
Research under Dr. Fan Yang

Hi, I am currently a senior at Wake Forest University, conducting research under Dr. Fan Yang. My research interests center on how machine learning can help accelerate and automate scientific processes.

Currently, I am investigating how we can learn “research taste” in mathematics and how agents can represent their understanding of a given system over extended periods of exploration and learning.

I believe that future research in AI for Science will increasingly focus on agentic systems capable of sustained exploration, abstraction, and learning over long time horizons.

I especially enjoy developing positional arguments about these topics and discussing them at conferences. If any of my work looks interesting, please reach out — I would love to chat.
